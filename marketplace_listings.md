Use these as product-card descriptions, detailed asset descriptions, or demo voiceover text.

## Public Infrastructure Event Feed - Curated

Mission-ready feed of public reports about infrastructure disruptions, normalized into event type, region, severity, confidence, corroboration count, and source context. Designed for crisis monitoring, logistics planning, and leadership briefings.

- Certification: Certified
- Quality score: 94
- Refresh: Every 2 hours
- Access: Request via data basket
- Handling: Aggregated public-source intelligence; no raw personal data
- Best for: Situation reports, dashboards, trend analysis

## OSINT Source Registry

Governed inventory of approved public sources, including source category, collection method, license family, allowed use, review date, language, coverage region, and reliability score. Helps analysts understand whether a source is official, media, reference, humanitarian, commercial, or public-web derived.

- Certification: Certified
- Quality score: 97
- Refresh: Daily
- Access: Self-service for trained OSINT analysts
- Handling: Public metadata with license caveats
- Best for: Source selection, provenance checks, collection planning

## Source Reliability Scorecard

Internal scorecard measuring source reliability using historical accuracy, timeliness, corroboration rate, correction history, and steward review. Supports explainable source weighting without exposing restricted collection details.

- Certification: Certified
- Quality score: 91
- Refresh: Weekly
- Access: Approved analysts and stewards
- Handling: Internal use only
- Best for: Confidence scoring, analytic tradecraft, source comparison
